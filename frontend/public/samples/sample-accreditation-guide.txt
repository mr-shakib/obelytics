DEMO ACCREDITATION GUIDE (SYNTHETIC)

This is a short synthetic guide written for demonstrating Obelytics. It is not the text of any accreditation body. Upload your accreditor's actual manual for real use.

CRITERION 1 PROGRAM OUTCOMES

The program shall state outcomes that describe what graduates are able to do, and shall map each course outcome to the program outcomes it supports. The mapping shall show how strongly each course contributes.

Every program outcome shall be addressed by more than one course, so that its attainment does not depend on a single assessment.

CRITERION 2 ASSESSMENT AND ATTAINMENT

The program shall measure the attainment of course outcomes directly, from students' performance in assessments mapped to those outcomes, and shall report the share of students who reach the attainment threshold.

The program shall show that its assessments test the outcomes and cognitive levels they are mapped to. Assessments that test a different outcome or a lower level than declared weaken the evidence of attainment.

CRITERION 3 CONTINUOUS QUALITY IMPROVEMENT

When an outcome is not attained, the program shall analyse the cause, record the actions it takes, and check in the following offering whether the actions worked.

Evidence of improvement shall include the analysis, the actions agreed by the teaching staff, and the results after the change.

CRITERION 4 CURRICULUM RELEVANCE

The program shall review its curriculum against the needs of employers and the profession, and shall show how the review led to changes in course content or outcomes.
